Class President by Accident

by Pau Garcia

Synopsis: Ria only wanted the free snacks. Now she runs the school.

Chapter 1: Vote for Cookies

Ria wrote her name on the sign-up sheet because it was next to the cookie plate. She did not read the sheet.

Three days later, her face was on every poster. Her slogan was just a photo of a cookie.
