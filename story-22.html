The Empty Chair

by Hana Mercado

Synopsis: One chair at the dinner table stays empty. Everyone pretends not to notice.

Chapter 1: Set for Five

Mama set five plates again tonight. Nobody said anything; they just ate quietly.

Lucas finally pulled out the empty chair and sat in it. "Dad would want us to laugh," he said. And they did.
