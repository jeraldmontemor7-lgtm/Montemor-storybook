Zero Hour Call

by Victor Kane

Synopsis: A negotiator has ten minutes to stop a stranger on the phone.

Chapter 1: Ten Minutes

Agent Park's phone rang in the empty office. "You have ten minutes," a voice said. "Then the city goes dark."

She started the timer. "Tell me your name," she said calmly, already tracing the call.
