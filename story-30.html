The Queen's Secret Garden

by Marguerite Bell

Synopsis: A gardener discovers the queen's hidden door.

Chapter 1: Behind the Ivy

Odette trimmed the ivy and found a small iron door. It was warm, as if someone had just left.

Inside bloomed flowers that should not exist. A voice behind her said, 'Please keep my secret.'
