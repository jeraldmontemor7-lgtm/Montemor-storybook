Crown of Moss

by Elowen Hart

Synopsis: A forest chooses a ruler, and it picks the wrong child.

Chapter 1: The Green Choosing

Moss crept over Pip's boots as the ancient trees bowed. The elders gasped; the forest had never chosen a stable boy.

Pip lifted the crown of living moss. It whispered, "Do not fear. We have waited for someone kind."
