Letters to Lia

by Mara Santos

Synopsis: A shy mailman falls for the girl whose letters he delivers.

Chapter 1: Address Unknown

Theo had delivered Lia's mail for a year without ever seeing her face. Her handwriting was his favorite part of the day.

One morning a letter arrived with no stamp, addressed to him. Inside was one line: "Meet me at the blue door."
