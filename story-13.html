Last Signal from Mars

by Orion Vale

Synopsis: A lone astronaut receives a message from a colony that was never built.

Chapter 1: Static

Commander Zhao was alone on Mars Base Delta when the radio clicked on. A calm voice said, "Welcome home."

There was no colony and no crew. Yet outside, a thousand lights glowed across the red dunes.
