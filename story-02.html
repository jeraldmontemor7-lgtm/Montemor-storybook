by Cleo Ramirez

Synopsis: Two strangers share one umbrella and one very long afternoon.

Chapter 1: Sudden Showers

Nina ran under the bus shelter just as the sky opened. A boy with a crooked grin lifted his umbrella and asked if she wanted to share.

They missed three buses on purpose. By evening, neither wanted the rain to stop.
