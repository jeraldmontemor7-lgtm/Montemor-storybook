Lanterns of Old Manila

by Isabel Reyes

Synopsis: A girl lights lanterns at night for the city's walled gates.

Chapter 1: Intramuros at Dusk

In 1890, Inés climbed the wall each dusk with a taper. One by one, the lanterns glowed along the stone.

A stranger watched from below. "You light the city," he said. "Who lights you?"
