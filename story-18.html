Synopsis: A pony express rider must cross the canyon before sunset.

Chapter 1: Ride at Dawn

Maya tightened the saddle straps. The mail had to reach Dry Creek before dark, and the bridge was gone.

Her horse snorted. Ahead lay only the canyon, wide as the sky. "Hold on," she whispered, and rode.
