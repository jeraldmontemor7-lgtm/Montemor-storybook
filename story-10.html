The Whispering Well

by Morgan Blackwood

Synopsis: Something in the old well knows your name.

Chapter 1: Echo

Every child in Alder Creek knew not to lean over the well. Sam did anyway and shouted his name.

The well answered in his own voice, but a second later than it should. Then it whispered, "Come closer."
