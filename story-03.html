Synopsis: An old cafe, a broken record player, and a second chance.

Chapter 1: One More Song

Cafe Luna was closing forever tonight. Mia swept the floor while a familiar song crackled from the old player.

The door opened. Ben, her first love, stood there holding two tickets to nowhere. "One last dance?" he asked.
