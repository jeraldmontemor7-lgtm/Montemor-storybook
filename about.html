About StoryNest

StoryNest is a Wattpad-inspired storybook website built for ITE 1. Every book has a single chapter, a synopsis, and a supporting illustration.

Features

  • 30 original single-chapter stories in 10 genres
  • Dark / light mode
  • Like button that remembers your favorites
  • Show / hide synopsis and reading progress bar
  • Genre filter and title search
