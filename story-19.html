My Cat Runs for Mayor

by Bea Lopez

Synopsis: Mr. Whiskers is winning the election by doing absolutely nothing.

Chapter 1: Paws for Office

It began when Mr. Whiskers napped on the town hall podium. The crowd cheered; he had never broken a promise.

His campaign slogan: "Meow." The polls said 62 percent. His opponent was a very confident goose.
